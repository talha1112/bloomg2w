Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 4, 2026  •  Last Updated: October 4, 2026

Project Bloom Girls 2 Women ("Project Bloom Girls 2 Women," "Project Bloom," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information of individuals who use our website, mobile applications, programs, services, and related platforms (collectively, the "Services").

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, store, protect, and delete information in connection with the Project Bloom Girls 2 Women mobile application (the "App") and our related Services.

By using the Services, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please do not use the Services.

1. About Project Bloom Girls 2 Women

Project Bloom Girls 2 Women is a nonprofit organization focused on empowering girls and women through education, coaching, connection, skill-building, personal development, community programs, and supportive experiences.

Our Services may include virtual Zoom sessions, workshops, membership programs, rewards, community partner opportunities, donations, educational activities, and related programs.

Our website is: https://projectbloomgirls2women.org/

  • IP address
  • Authentication information

  • Basic diagnostic or technical information

F. Notifications

If you grant permission for notifications, we may process a device or push-notification token so that we can send relevant notifications, including:

  • Session reminders
  • Program updates
  • Registration updates
  • Reward-related notifications
  • Account-related notifications
  • Important service communications

You can manage notification permissions through your device settings.

3. Information Relating to Children and Teen Participants

Project Bloom Girls 2 Women provides programs that may be available to girls and young participants, including participants between the ages of 8 and 17.

For participants under 18, participation may require parental or legal guardian permission or other appropriate authorization, depending on the program and applicable requirements.

We take additional care with information relating to minors.

We do not knowingly require children to provide more personal information than is reasonably necessary to participate in an applicable program or service.

Where appropriate, we may collect information necessary to:

  • Create and manage a participant account
  • Confirm program participation
  • Manage session registration
  • Record attendance
  • Provide program services
  • Manage badges and rewards
  • Communicate important program information
  • Maintain safety and security
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

Parents or legal guardians may contact us regarding a minor's personal information or account.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information without appropriate authorization, please contact us using the information in the Contact Us section.
