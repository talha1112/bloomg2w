Delete Account & Data

Project Bloom Girls 2 Women — Bloom G2W App

This page explains how users of the Bloom G2W app, published by Project Bloom Girls 2 Women, can request deletion of their account and the personal data associated with it.

How to Request Deletion

You can request deletion in either of the two ways below.

Option 1 — In the App (fastest)

  1. Open the Bloom G2W app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile & Settings.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

Your account and associated data are deleted immediately — no waiting period, no further action needed.

Option 2 — By Email (no app access needed)

  1. Email info@projectbloomgirls2women.com from the email address associated with your Bloom G2W account.
  2. Use the subject line "Account Deletion Request" and include the full name on the account.
  3. We will verify your identity and delete your account and associated data, normally within 7 business days, and confirm by email once complete.

Security/fraud-prevention logs (e.g. payment webhook logs)Kept — operational records not tied to your profile, retained as required for fraud prevention and legal compliance

Anonymized and retained records no longer identify you personally once your account is deleted.

Retention Period

Account and profile data is deleted immediately upon request (or within 7 business days for email requests). Anonymized financial/accounting records and non-personal operational logs described above are retained only as long as required for accounting, legal, or fraud-prevention purposes, consistent with our Privacy Policy.

Deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone. If you only want some of your data deleted without deleting your entire account, contact us using the email above and describe what you'd like removed.
